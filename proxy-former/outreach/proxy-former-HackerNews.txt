TITLE:
ProxyFormer: Proxy tokens for 700K-token training on one 16GB GPU

URL:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23463

FIRST COMMENT (post immediately after submitting, as the author):
Author here, independent researcher.

The idea: in every layer, compress each chunk of tokens into a "proxy", run global attention only among the proxies, then inject the result back into the full-resolution token stream. That stream persists across layers, so nothing is thrown away for good. With ratio r, attention cost is (1/r)^2 and decoding keeps only a proxy KV cache of length L/r.

Numbers from the paper (small models, d_model 512, one 16 GB GPU, batch 1):
- ~21K tokens of history: 15.6 GB -> 2.9 GB of VRAM, 1.3 -> 16.1 it/s vs a full-attention baseline
- trainable length 716,800 tokens, about 35x a standard decoder on the same card
- 50-needle retrieval, trained on 64K: 100% at 128K, 99-100% at 256K, 92-95% at 1M
- WikiText-103 perplexity 21.01 with compressed history (21.36 for the decoder-only baseline without history)

It's implemented with plain reshape/Linear/Conv and standard SDPA, with no custom kernels. It's not a SOTA claim; I don't have the compute to test it at scale.
